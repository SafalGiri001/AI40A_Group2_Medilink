About MediLink

Simplifying Healthcare Access in One Centralized Platform

MediLink is a web-based healthcare accessibility platform built to help patients, families, elderly users, students, and visitors quickly locate nearby medical services, manage personal health records, and make informed decisions when time matters.

6 Pillars Discovery, Maps, Emergency, Records, Reminders & MediBot
24/7 Access Centralized digital prescriptions, reports & emergency QR
Built in Nepal Aligned with person-centred digital health goals

The Problem We Solve

Healthcare information is often scattered across disconnected sources, while paper and electronic records run in parallel. During an emergency or in an unfamiliar area, patients struggle to identify which hospital or trauma center is nearby, what services are available, how fast they can reach it, or where their past prescriptions and reports are stored.

Our Aim & Vision

MediLink brings verified facility directories, proximity-based navigation, emergency contacts, first-aid steps, personal health records, and plain-language AI guidance together under one roof—empowering individuals to navigate healthcare with clarity and confidence.

What MediLink Offers

Designed around everyday healthcare navigation and emergency readiness.

Provider Search & Profiles

Search and filter hospitals and doctors by specialty, condition, rating, experience, consultation fees, and available time slots.

Interactive Maps & Navigation

Filter hospitals, pharmacies, and trauma centers by distance and get turn-by-turn directions with estimated travel times.

Emergency Support & QR

Access tap-to-call emergency numbers, step-by-step first-aid guides, and a revocable Emergency QR Code with vital medical info.

Health Records & Prescriptions

Store PDF/image medical reports, track chronological health history, log digital prescriptions, and check for potential drug conflicts.

Calendar & Medicine Reminders

Manage check-ups and medication schedules with dose adherence tracking (taken/skipped) and real-time in-app notifications.

MediBot AI Assistant

Get instant platform help, plain-language explanations of medical terms, and immediate emergency contacts when urgent keywords appear.

Important Scope & Clinical Boundary

MediLink is an informational and personal health-organization platform. We do not replace licensed doctors, hospitals, or emergency responders, and we do not provide medical diagnoses, clinical treatments, appointment booking transactions, or medical billing. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

HOW IT IS BUILT

Modern, Modular Architecture

Developed collaboratively using Agile Scrum sprints by our six-member engineering team, MediLink combines a responsive HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Jinja frontend with a Python Flask backend, MySQL relational database, Google Maps Platform, and AI API integration.

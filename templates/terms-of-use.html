Terms of Use

Last updated: November 2026 · MediLink Healthcare Accessibility Platform

Medical & Emergency Disclaimer: MediLink provides healthcare facility information and personal record organization only. It does not provide medical diagnosis, clinical treatment, or professional medical advice. In a life-threatening emergency, call local emergency services or visit the nearest hospital immediately.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or creating an account on MediLink, you agree to follow these Terms of Use and our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the platform.

2. Platform Scope & Limitations

MediLink acts solely as a centralized healthcare discovery and personal health-record organizer. Specifically:

  • No Clinical Care: Hospitals, doctors, and pharmacies listed on MediLink operate independently. MediLink does not practice medicine or supervise clinical decisions.
  • No Booking or Payment Processing: Doctor availability slots and consultation fees are displayed for informational purposes; MediLink does not process hospital bookings or financial payments.
  • Directory Accuracy: While administrators regularly maintain facility profiles, operating hours, and contact numbers, users should confirm urgent availability directly with the healthcare provider.

3. MediBot AI & Prescription Conflict Checker

  • MediBot Assistant: MediBot offers general health education, platform guidance, and plain-language explanations of medical terms. AI-generated responses can occasionally be incomplete or inaccurate and must never replace consultation with a licensed physician.
  • Prescription Conflict Alerts: Automated conflict warnings compare your recorded prescriptions against reference drug-interaction data. Always verify medication changes or potential interactions with a doctor or pharmacist before starting or stopping any medicine.

4. User Accounts & Responsibilities

  • You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials and signing out on shared devices.
  • When uploading medical reports or entering prescriptions, you confirm that you have the right to store those records and that uploaded files are lawful, non-malicious PDFs or images.
  • You are responsible for reviewing the details stored in your Emergency QR Code and revoking it if you no longer wish it to be accessible.

5. Reviews, Ratings & Acceptable Use

Users may submit ratings and reviews for hospitals and doctors as well as feedback on MediLink. You agree not to:

  • Post false, defamatory, abusive, or spam reviews, or share another person's private medical information.
  • Attempt to bypass authentication, scrape directory data, upload malware, or disrupt platform availability.

MediLink administrators reserve the right to moderate or remove reviews and suspend accounts that violate these terms.

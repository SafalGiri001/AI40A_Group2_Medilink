Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Effective for MediLink Web Platform

Your health information stays private and secure. MediLink only collects the data needed to help you locate healthcare facilities, manage your personal health records, and receive reminders.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use MediLink, we may collect and store the following categories of information:

  • Account & Profile Details: Your name, email address, securely hashed password, and optional multi-factor authentication settings.
  • Personal Health Records: Medical reports (PDFs or images) you upload to the Medical Report Vault, chronological diagnoses/treatments, and digital prescription records (medicine names, dosages, and instructions).
  • Schedules & Reminders: Appointment dates, follow-up reminders, medicine schedules, and dose adherence logs (taken or skipped).
  • Emergency QR Data: Critical medical details you explicitly choose to include in your Emergency QR Code.
  • Location & Search Queries: Approximate or device-shared location data used solely to calculate distances, filter nearby hospitals/pharmacies/trauma centers, and generate directions.
  • MediBot Chat Sessions & Feedback: Questions entered into MediBot during active sessions, along with hospital/doctor reviews and platform feedback you submit.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account, prevent unauthorized login attempts, and automatically terminate inactive sessions.
  • Display nearby healthcare providers, estimated travel times, and turn-by-turn routes.
  • Organize your health history, check for potential conflicts among your recorded prescriptions, and trigger medicine or follow-up notifications.
  • Provide general health information, platform navigation help, and plain-language explanations of medical terms through MediBot.

3. Third-Party Services & APIs

To deliver core functionality, MediLink integrates trusted external services:

  • Google Maps Platform: Receives location coordinates or search queries to display map markers, proximity distances, and directions.
  • MediBot AI Provider: Processes your chat messages to return general health information. Please avoid entering sensitive personal identifiers (such as full name, citizenship ID, or contact numbers) inside MediBot prompts.
  • Email Delivery Service: Used strictly for account verification and time-limited password reset links.

4. Security Measures

MediLink protects user accounts and health records using cryptographic password hashing, encrypted HTTPS transport, access-controlled file storage for uploaded reports, session invalidation upon logout or inactivity timeout, and regular database backups.

5. Your Rights & Control

  • Access & Export: View your health history anytime and export selected medical reports for offline use or sharing with your doctor.
  • Edit & Delete: Update profile details, edit or delete your provider reviews, and permanently remove uploaded reports or prescriptions from your vault.
  • QR Code Revocation: Regenerate or revoke your Emergency QR Code at any time so previously shared codes stop displaying your emergency summary.
